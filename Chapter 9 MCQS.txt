Chapter section: 1.3 A human in the loop is not a gate (who, what, when)
Mehwish manages reservations at a 120-seat restaurant. A guest was charged a Rs 5,000 deposit twice, and she wants Claude to draft the apology and refund reply from the booking and payment record. The restaurant stays responsible for anything it tells a guest about their money, and the email goes out at the end of the shift. Her team lead says only, “We’ll keep a human in the loop.” Which gate is written most appropriately?
A. The evening shift will read the reply and make sure it looks fine before it is sent out.
B. The duty manager checks the refund amount and date against the payment record before sending.
C. The finance team reviews a weekly sample of sent replies to confirm the amounts were correct.
D. The reservations manager rereads each reply for a polite, apologetic tone before it is emailed.
Correct Answer: Option B
Explanation (Roman Urdu): Option B sahi hai kyunke is mein teeno cheezein hain: kaun check karta hai (duty manager), kya check karta hai (amount aur date payment record se) aur kab (bhejne se pehle). Scenario mein asal risk paison ke baare mein ghalat fact ka hai, aur B usi risk ko pakadta hai. A sirf “someone will check” hai, yani intention hai, control nahi. C bhejne ke baad check karta hai, jab ghalti wapas nahi hoti (wrong timing). D sirf tone dekhta hai, jabke asal risk facts ka hai (wrong risk).
Yaad rakhne wala rule: Gate = kaun, kya verify kare, aur kab (undo hone se pehle).
